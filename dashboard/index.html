How the implied value is built

implied = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × reach

Every factor comes from this snapshot, not from a list of names. The category multiple is the median market-cap / TVL of live-token protocols in the same category; a category with fewer than three live comparables falls back to the global median. Growth is the 7-day TVL change, clamped, worth at most ±30%. Maturity runs from 0.70 for a protocol listed yesterday to 1.00 after three years. Reach adds 4% per extra chain, up to +20%. Move the sliders to see how sensitive the ranking is — the board above uses the defaults.

What this radar does — and does not — claim

  • “Records no token” is a snapshot fact, not a promise. It means the snapshot's fields for that protocol are all empty. A token could exist under another name, or launch tomorrow.
  • Implied value is this entry's model. It says what a protocol would be worth if it traded at its category's median multiple. It is not a price, a prediction, or a guarantee of an airdrop.
  • Farm plans are generic playbooks — a fresh wallet, the protocol's main product, a weekly cadence, a 30-day hold, and a written log. They cost real gas on real chains; the costs shown are per-transaction estimates for the protocol's main chain.
  • Live-token protocols are set aside in their own tab. They are the comparables the multiples come from, not the radar's targets.
  • Data: DefiLlama protocol index, snapshot 2026-11-14. No external data is fetched by this page.
